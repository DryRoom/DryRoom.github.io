Never have I ever expected a project which we (me and a friend) made just to score some marks, get our professor's attention that they selected it to be displayed as an exhibit. Well, this isn't an exhibition where little kids come to see how 'cool' we made it and to be honest its the exact opposite. Here industrial intellectuals arrive to do an annual seminar with our college directors and at the same time if a project gets their attention, they'll propose a deal to continue the project's research.
The whole event took for like 3 hours leaving a few hours for the preparation and last minute adjustments. Thankfully, our college gave a shit for our hunger and treated us with Chowmein and 'infinite' coffee (idk why my friends keep telling me they tasted a bit orange in it).
Nevertheless the event was enjoyable, explaining, answering, nobody got a deal but who cares? as long as we had fun we can keep money aside.

Btw I would be publishing our project source code in my repo if anybody wants, I would probably explain our project in detail there.
